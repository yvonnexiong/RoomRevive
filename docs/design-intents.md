# Design Intents

## 1. Calm & Unwind

Goal:
Reduce noise, create calm

Changes:
- warm soft lighting
- minimal objects (1–2)
- subtle steam / oven glow
- matte cabinet feel
- centered layout

---

## 2. Host & Gather

Goal:
Encourage connection and warmth

Changes:
- brighter warm lighting
- table setup (2–4 settings)
- glasses / social props
- layered visuals
- slight asymmetry

---

## 3. Fast & Focused

Goal:
Maximize efficiency

Changes:
- neutral/cool lighting
- tools visible
- active cooking cues
- structured layout
- high clarity

---

## Transformation Hierarchy

High impact:
- lighting
- island perception
- appliance usage

Medium:
- cabinet finish
- stools
- surface styling

Low:
- decor
