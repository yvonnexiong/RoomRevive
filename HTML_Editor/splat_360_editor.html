SPLAT 360

Load a Gaussian-splat scene, walk to a spot, and bake a 360° equirectangular panorama from that exact position.

Drop .ply / .spz to view.

Capture point

Double-click any surface in the view to drop the capture point there (X/Z from the hit, Y = floor + eye height). The green sphere marks where the panorama is shot from.

Panorama

Renders 6 cube faces from the capture point and stitches them into an equirectangular PNG (2:1). Saves alongside as panorama_x_y_z.png — drop it in HTML_Editor/Panoramas/.

Room crop

Optional: clip away splats outside the room so the panorama has clean walls instead of exterior junk.

Floaters

–

Voxelises space into cell-sized cubes, counts opaque (alpha > solid) splats per cell, and flags any splat whose 3×3×3 neighbourhood holds fewer than max density splats as an isolated floater. Cell 5–8 cm and max density ≈15 suit room-scale kitchens. Max density is the main dial — raise to cut more fuzz. Applies to the live view and the 360 render.
