APPLIANCE boxes

Separate 3D bounding boxes for the fridge, oven and microwave, detected with the Boxer pipeline. Floaters are hidden by default.

Box fit

Nudges each box's left, right and front faces inward until they sit this far from the nearest (non-floater) splat. Updates all boxes live. Floaters are excluded from the test.

Floor node grid

–
–

Grid of free-space waypoints at floor + height inside the crop box, spaced evenly. A point is a node if it isn't inside a splat and has clear space (no splat within clearance) in at least one of N/S/E/W.

Detection backend

YOLO World: fast real-time detector, open-vocab classes.

K-node placement

0

Hover a surface and press K: drops count green nodes on a half-circle (arc span) at a constant distance from the hit point. The radius grows from Radius outward until a node hits a splat, capped at Max placement. Pressing K again moves them; editing any value re-places them at the last K spot.
