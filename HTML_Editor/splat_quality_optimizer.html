Splat Quality Optimizer

Upload a .spz or .ply, dial in the cleanup, and export a polished .spz that renders sharper on Quest 3. Spherical-harmonic colour is preserved.

Cleanup  ·  remove bad splats

Deletes isolated specks whose 3×3×3 neighbourhood holds fewer than min neighbours opaque splats. The #1 cause of fuzzy edges. Raise min neighbours to cut more.

Removes near-transparent splats that only add blend noise and sorting cost.

Deletes giant stretched "spike" gaussians that smear across surfaces and edges.

Smoothing  ·  reshape survivors

Caps how large any single splat can be, so big blobby gaussians sit flush against the surface instead of bulging over edges.

Fattens thin "needle" splats so their longest axis is at most this many times their shortest. Needles are a major edge-shimmer source on mobile.

Density  ·  fewer = better sort

Keeps only the strongest splat per cell. Fewer splats let Quest depth-sort every frame more accurately → less popping. Larger cell = lighter & smoother, but softer detail.
